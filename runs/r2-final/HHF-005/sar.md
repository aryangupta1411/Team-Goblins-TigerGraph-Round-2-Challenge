This Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) addresses an undocumented coordinated fraud pattern, case AC-HHF-005. The subjects are customer C00348 and debit card C00348-K1, which is connected to 16 other cards in a micro-skimming wave. On 2017-01-28, customer C00348 denied an online transaction 3660250 for $4.05, a Product C purchase in billing region 264.0. Investigation revealed this transaction is part of a series of six sub-$6 online Product C charges on card C00348-K1, starting on 2017-01-07 at 15:32:31 for $4.30 in region 325.0 (transaction 3660245), followed by $5.08 on 2017-01-15 at 22:51:07 (3660246), $2.70 on 2017-01-16 at 14:04:39 (3660247), $4.94 on 2017-01-18 at 19:30:07 (3660248), and $2.10 on 2017-01-20 at 11:03:40 (3660249), all in region 264.0, culminating in the denied transaction 3660250. This multi-day sub-$6 online Product C micro-charge skimming wave across 17 cards links card C00348-K1 to 16 other cards (C00129-K1, C01326-K2, C03427-K2, C03488-K1, C03611-K2, C04274-K1, C04528-K1, C05083-K1, C05263-K2, C06198-K1, C09471-K2, C10035-K2, C10654-K1, C11332-K1, C12975-K2, C13290-K1) targeted in the same January 2017 micro-skimming wave. The amounts of these online transactions, ranging from $2.10 to $5.08, are significantly below the card's prior median transaction amount of $58.98 and prior maximum of $774.08 over 149 transactions, which is unusual for the cardholder's historical spending. No prior suspicious activity report has been filed on this card. OFAC screening of the customer and card identifiers against the SDN list returned no match. A fraud case was opened and connected cards are being monitored, while a block of card C00348-K1 is recommended and awaits team-lead approval. This report is recommended and awaiting fraud-manager approval, covering a total suspicious amount of $23.17.
