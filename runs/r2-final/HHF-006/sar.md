This report details an undocumented coordinated fraud pattern identified in internal case AC-HHF-006, opened on 2017-01-28 following an analyst request. The customer C01727, holding Visa debit card C01727-K2, is involved. Card C01727-K2 is connected to 16 other cards, C00534-K2, C01357-K1, C01507-K1, C01594-K1, C02167-K2, C03060-K1, C04146-K1, C04668-K1, C05610-K1, C06115-K1, C06639-K1, C08384-K1, C10350-K1, C11617-K1, C11743-K2, and C11940-K1, through a shared recipient email domain. On 2017-01-17 at 15:29, transaction 3660261 for $305.41 was an online purchase with product code C in billing region 231.0. This was followed by transaction 3660262 on 2017-01-18 at 19:40 for $366.35, also an online purchase with product code C in billing region 231.0. The flagged transaction 3660263 occurred on 2017-01-28 at 07:59 for $295.92, an online purchase with product code C in billing region 231.0. All three transactions shared the recipient email domain 'sc.rr.com', which is identified as a shared origin element linking C01727-K2 to the aforementioned 16 other cards through online Product Code C transactions within 30 days before the case opened. This activity is suspicious as it represents a coordinated recipient-email mule ring across multiple cards, which does not align with the cardholder's typical activity, despite the amounts being within the card's baseline (prior median $107.86, prior maximum $1,419.28 over 121 transactions), and the region 231.0 being the card's modal region (120 prior transactions). A prior suspicious activity report was filed on this card under closed cases CC-1975, CC-1833, CC-1501. OFAC screening of the customer and card identifiers against the SDN list returned no match. Internal case AC-HHF-006 was opened, monitoring of connected cards was initiated, and escalation to an analyst was completed. A block of card C01727-K2 is recommended and awaits team-lead (L1) approval, and this report filing is recommended, awaiting fraud-manager (L2) approval, for a total suspicious amount of $967.68.
